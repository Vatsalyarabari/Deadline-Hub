Deadline Hub · every deadline, in one place

Connect your school

Deadline Hub reads your courses and course files, finds every due date, and puts them in your calendar with reminders. Everything stays on this computer.

  1. Your browser opened your school's Moodle. Sign in if it asks.
  2. When the browser asks to open Deadline Hub (or "Moodle"), click Open.
  3. Come back here. You'll be connected in a few seconds.

Didn't work? Other ways to connect

Or use the browser extension. By hand:

  1. In Chrome or Edge, sign in to your Moodle. Press F12 → Network → tick Preserve log.
  2. In that tab open: pick your school above
  3. Right-click the red token= row → Copy → Copy URL, paste below.
  1. Open your Canvas settings (Account → Settings).
  2. Under Approved Integrations, click + New Access Token. Purpose: Deadline Hub. Leave the expiry blank or pick the end of term.
  3. Copy the token Canvas shows (it's only shown once) and paste it here.
Where do I find it?
  • D2L / Brightspace: Calendar → Subscribe (top right) → copy the link.
  • Blackboard: Calendar → ⋯ → Share calendar → copy the link.
  • Moodle: Calendar → Export calendar → All courses → Get calendar URL.
  • Canvas: Calendar → Calendar Feed (bottom right).
  • Google Classroom: Google Calendar → the class calendar's Settings → "Secret address in iCal format".
Calendar links only include due dates your instructors added to the calendar, not dates inside files.

Connected apps

Notion · cards on your task board
  1. Go to notion.so/profile/integrations → New integration, copy the token.
  2. Open your task board → ••• → Connections → add the integration. Your board needs a Date column.
  3. Copy the board's link (••• → Copy link) and paste both here.
Todoist · tasks with phone reminders
  1. Todoist → Settings → Integrations → Developer → copy your API token.

Checking something off in Notion or Todoist checks it off here too, and the other way around.
